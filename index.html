Method

Projection

Ratings blend this season with last, weighted by games played. Each factor is converted to points.

poss = paceH × paceA ÷ lg pace
pts = poss × (ORtg + opp DRtg − lg) ÷ 100
injuries = (BPM+2) × MPG/48 per player out
rest: B2B −1.5 · 3-in-4 −0.8 · travel −0.5
home +2.0 · altitude +1.0

Props: minutes × per-minute rate × usage bump from teammates out × pace × opponent defense.

Market check

Fair line = 35% model + 65% Pinnacle (40/60 for props). EV is priced at FanDuel.

Line movement from open, reverse line movement and money-vs-ticket splits confirm or downgrade a play. They never create one.
